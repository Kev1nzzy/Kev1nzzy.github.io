Graph-Rationale-Guided RAG for Medical QA

A retrieval framework that combines UMLS–Neo4j knowledge graphs, multi-source retrieval, hallucination detection and QLoRA fine-tuning to give more grounded answers.

+10–12%accuracy
~20%fewer hallucinations
>80%graph fidelity

Evaluated on MedQA, MedMCQA and RareDisease-MedQuAD. Published in Academic Journal of Science and Technology, Vol. 19, No. 2 (2026), pp. 321–331. Developed in an LLM research seminar supervised by Dr. Pavlos Protopapas (Harvard). A research system, not a clinical product.
